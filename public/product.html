Frequently asked questions

These questions should be easy for the platform to answer through its behavior, not just documentation.

Where should a new customer start?

A new customer should start with account creation, legal acceptance, package activation, contractor record setup, and then proceed into payment and bookkeeping activity.

Why are reports blank?

Reports should only populate after the underlying contractor records, payment events, and bookkeeping transactions have been entered.

Why is a feature unavailable?

The system should make it clear whether the blocker is caused by missing subscription status, missing package entitlement, or missing operational data.

Why is access restricted after billing changes?

Access should remain aligned to actual package status so unpaid, canceled, or downgraded accounts do not retain unrestricted use of available product features.

Can one company see another company’s data?

No. Tenant isolation should ensure that operational data, reports, and exports remain scoped to the the right company context.
