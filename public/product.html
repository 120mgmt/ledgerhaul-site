Expected workflow sequence

The product flow should move customers through a disciplined operational path instead of placing blank reporting screens at the front of the experience.

1

Create contractor records

Establish contractor master data, tax status, and W-9 readiness before any compensation activity is entered.

2

Record payments and bookkeeping

Enter contractor payment events and bookkeeping transactions as the operational source data used by downstream reporting.

3

Review reports and exports

Use 1099 summary, contractor exports, and ledger reporting only after underlying operational records have been captured and reviewed.

Control requirements

Revenue protection and platform integrity depend on billing enforcement, tenant isolation, feature entitlement controls, and authenticated access gates across all application pages and endpoints.

Subscription enforcement

Workspace and report access should be revoked automatically for canceled, unpaid, expired, or downgraded accounts.

Feature entitlements

Customers should only access contractors, payments, bookkeeping, and reporting tools that are included in the assigned package.

Tenant-scoped access

All operations should remain locked to authenticated company context so customers only see and manage their own records and exports.
