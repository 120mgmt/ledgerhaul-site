Core platform capabilities

LedgerHaul is not limited to a single industry. It provides universal payroll and financial infrastructure, while offering advanced features for organizations with complex operational and cost structures.

Flexible Payroll & Compensation

Support hourly, salary, flat rate, percentage, mileage, and CPM-based compensation. Manage deductions, reimbursements, and generate detailed, audit-ready pay statements.

Advanced Cost & Margin Intelligence

Model true operating costs using fixed and variable inputs. For logistics use cases, apply cost-per-mile and route-level analysis to evaluate profitability before committing resources.

Billing, Invoicing & Subscriptions

Integrated billing infrastructure with subscriptions, invoicing, customer portals, and secure payment processing.

Compliance & Audit Controls

Enforce legal acceptance, track compliance checkpoints, and maintain audit-ready financial and payroll records across the platform.

Reporting & Reconciliation

Generate reconciled financial reports across payroll, billing, and profitability with consistent data models and clear visibility.

Logistics-Specific Enhancements

Optional capabilities for transportation and logistics operations, including settlement-style pay, mileage economics, and operational profitability analysis.
