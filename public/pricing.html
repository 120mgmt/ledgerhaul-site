Why this structure works

The package ladder supports direct conversion, clearer buyer fit, and stronger control over access after payment is confirmed.

Conversion

Direct Stripe flow

Package buttons should open checkout for first purchase instead of pushing new users into billing portal screens meant for existing subscribers.

Fit

Cleaner buyer match

Bookkeeping-only buyers can purchase exactly what they need, while payroll buyers can choose payroll-only or the combined premium package.

Control

Protected app access

Active subscription status should gate the software so customers cannot use app workspaces, reports, or exports before actually paying.

Buy a plan first. Then unlock the software.

The correct flow is sign up, sign in, select package, complete Stripe checkout, confirm subscription through webhook and access checks, then allow dashboard entry. No active subscription should mean no software access.
