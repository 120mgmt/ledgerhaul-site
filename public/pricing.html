FAQ

Can I start payroll-only and upgrade later?

Yes. The pricing is designed for payroll-first adoption. Add bookkeeping when you’re ready.

Does this lock me into one payroll vendor?

No. The platform is built provider-agnostic. Provider adapters are isolated so switching is controlled.

Do I need to pay for bookkeeping to run payroll?

No. Payroll runs independently. Bookkeeping is an add-on for teams who want full finance workflows.
