Pricing structure

Start with a lower-friction entry point and scale revenue by operational depth.

The pricing model should make it easy for a contractor-focused customer to say yes quickly, then expand into higher-value bookkeeping and broader financial operations as needs grow.

Lower entry barrier

1099-first customers should see an accessible starting price before encountering broader bookkeeping or payroll positioning.

Usage-based expansion

Per-contractor pricing supports revenue growth as customers increase headcount and payment activity.

Clear feature progression

Each package should add visible operational value instead of forcing advanced pricing too early.

Access control alignment

Package entitlements should match what the customer can access across contractors, payments, bookkeeping, and reporting.

Billing access

Billing access, package assignment, and workspace availability should remain tied to active subscription status and assigned feature entitlements.

Why this structure works

Leading at $39 creates an easier initial yes, while usage-based contractor pricing and operational add-ons grow monthly revenue as customers expand.

Stronger entry conversion

Starting at $39 is a more approachable front-door price than opening with a $99 contractor-only package before buyers understand the operational value.

Cleaner progression

Customers can move from basic contractor records and pay runs into advanced settlements, exports, and reporting without pricing confusion.

Higher-value upsell path

Bookkeeping and payroll should remain the premium position because those workflows create broader operational leverage and support higher pricing.

Subscription control requirements

Billing and access logic should enforce active subscription status, package entitlements, and downgrade restrictions across all workspaces and reporting tools.

Active subscription enforcement

Workspace and export access should be removed automatically for inactive, unpaid, canceled, or downgraded accounts.

Feature entitlement enforcement

Customers should only see and use the contractor, payment, bookkeeping, and reporting features included in the assigned package.

Operational continuity

Active customers should move directly from package selection into billing, sign-in, and workspace access without manual support intervention.
