Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LedgerHaul collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses information in connection with account creation, billing, contractor payment workflows, bookkeeping tools, reporting features, and related platform functionality.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through LedgerHaul websites, account registration, subscription workflows, application interfaces, communications, and related services. By using LedgerHaul, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

LedgerHaul may collect information you provide directly, information created through platform usage, and limited technical information associated with access and security.

  • Account registration details such as company name, administrator name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Billing and subscription information necessary to manage platform access and payment status.
  • Operational records entered into the platform, including contractor details, payment records, bookkeeping entries, and related business data.
  • Technical and device-related information such as browser type, approximate timestamps, session details, and log data used for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate the platform, authenticate users, manage subscriptions, enforce access controls, process operational workflows, improve service quality, respond to support requests, communicate account-related updates, and protect the integrity and security of LedgerHaul.

4. How Information May Be Shared

LedgerHaul does not sell your business data to advertisers. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with law, protect platform rights, respond to valid legal requests, or work with service providers supporting hosting, infrastructure, billing, security, analytics, communications, or other operational functions.

  • Service providers may receive limited information needed to perform contracted functions.
  • Information may be disclosed when required by law, court order, or valid governmental request.
  • Information may be disclosed to investigate fraud, misuse, security issues, or violations of platform terms.

5. Data Retention

LedgerHaul may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security and audit records, enforce agreements, resolve disputes, satisfy legal obligations, and support legitimate business operations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the data and applicable legal or operational requirements.

6. Security Measures

LedgerHaul uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no platform, network, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, and you remain responsible for protecting account credentials and restricting access to authorized users.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update, or request deletion of certain personal information. LedgerHaul may need to retain some records where required for legal, operational, billing, security, audit, or contractual reasons.

8. Third-Party Services

LedgerHaul may rely on third-party infrastructure, payment, authentication, communications, analytics, or other service providers. Use of those integrated services may also be subject to their own terms and privacy practices. LedgerHaul is not responsible for third-party policies outside its control.

9. Policy Updates

LedgerHaul may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions become effective when posted unless a different effective date is stated. Continued use of the platform after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or LedgerHaul information handling practices should be submitted through the appropriate business or support contact channel associated with the platform.
