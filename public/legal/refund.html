Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how LedgerHaul handles subscription billing, cancellation timing, non-refundable charges, and any approved refunds or credits.

Applies to paid subscriptions
Reviewed with account signup

Sections

1. Scope 2. Subscription Charges 3. Refund Eligibility 4. Non-Refundable Charges 5. Cancellations 6. Downgrades and Access Changes 7. Refund Processing 8. Contact and Review Requests

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to amounts charged for access to LedgerHaul subscriptions, packages, and related paid platform functionality. By purchasing or maintaining a paid subscription, you agree to this Refund Policy in addition to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

2. Subscription Charges

LedgerHaul may charge recurring subscription fees, package-based fees, user-based fees, contractor-based fees, or other approved charges tied to platform access and operational functionality. Charges are billed according to the subscription plan or package selected by the customer.

  • Billing may recur automatically until canceled in accordance with platform rules.
  • Access to certain workspaces or features may depend on successful payment and active subscription status.
  • Failure to pay may result in restricted or suspended access.

3. Refund Eligibility

Except where required by law or expressly approved by LedgerHaul in writing, subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started and platform access has been made available. Refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis for duplicate billing, proven billing errors, or other exceptional circumstances.

  • Any approved refund remains discretionary unless required by law.
  • Requests must include sufficient detail to identify the charge and reason for review.
  • Partial credits may be issued instead of full refunds where appropriate.

4. Non-Refundable Charges

Unless otherwise required by law, the following are generally non-refundable:

  • Subscription fees for billing periods already started.
  • Fees tied to activated or assigned feature access.
  • Charges associated with account inactivity caused by customer choice or failure to cancel before renewal.
  • Implementation, onboarding, customization, support, or setup work if separately offered and completed.

5. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription according to the cancellation process provided within LedgerHaul or through the applicable billing workflow. Cancellation generally stops future renewal charges but does not automatically create a refund for the current billing period unless otherwise required by law or expressly approved.

6. Downgrades and Access Changes

If your subscription is downgraded, canceled, unpaid, or otherwise restricted, access to certain features, reports, exports, or workspaces may be reduced or removed. Changes in access level do not by themselves create an automatic right to a refund for previously billed periods.

7. Refund Processing

If LedgerHaul approves a refund, the refund will generally be processed back to the original payment method when reasonably possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, card network, bank, or financial institution involved.

8. Contact and Review Requests

Refund or billing review requests should be submitted with the company name, account email, date of charge, amount charged, and a clear explanation of the issue. LedgerHaul may request additional information reasonably needed to review the request.
