Refund & Billing Policy

Effective Date: 2026-02-08
Company: LedgerHaul

Summary: LedgerHaul is a subscription software service. Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period begins. Fees tied to processing, payroll execution, filings facilitation, payment transmission, and third-party services are non-refundable once initiated.

1) Subscription Fees

Subscription fees are charged in advance for the billing period shown at checkout (monthly or annual, if offered). Unless required by law, subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing period begins, including in cases of partial use, early cancellation, or unused time.

2) Trials and First-Time Subscriptions

  • If a free or discounted trial is offered, you may cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges.
  • If you are charged because you did not cancel before the trial ends, refunds are not guaranteed.
  • Where required by applicable law, we will honor mandatory cancellation/refund rights.

3) Processing, Payroll, and Third-Party Fees

Certain Service features may initiate processing events, including but not limited to payment processing, ACH or banking network instructions, payroll execution, document generation, or filings facilitation via third-party providers.

All such fees are non-refundable once processing has commenced, including in cases where results are delayed, rejected, reversed, or impacted by third-party outages, bank rules, insufficient funds, incorrect input, authorization issues, or other conditions outside our control.

4) Upgrade / Downgrade

  • Upgrades may take effect immediately. Any proration is handled by the payment processor (e.g., Stripe) based on your plan configuration.
  • Downgrades generally take effect at the next renewal unless stated otherwise during plan change.
  • Plan changes do not automatically create refund rights for previously paid amounts.

5) Cancellation

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals, but does not automatically refund fees already paid. Your access remains active through the end of the current paid period unless your account is suspended for policy violations, non-payment, chargeback risk, fraud prevention, or legal compliance requirements.

6) Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute, we may suspend service access while the dispute is investigated. You remain responsible for amounts due under your subscription and for any fees or costs incurred by us as a result of the dispute. Repeated disputes, suspected fraud, or abuse indicators may result in account termination.

7) Exceptional Refunds (Case-by-Case)

We may consider a refund or credit in limited cases, at our discretion, for example:

  • Duplicate subscription charges confirmed for the same billing period.
  • Billing errors caused by a verified system malfunction supported by our logs.
  • Refunds required by applicable law.

Any approved refund is issued to the original payment method. Posting times depend on your bank or payment method and may take several business days.

8) Taxes and Governmental Fees

Taxes, governmental fees, and third-party processing fees are generally non-refundable unless required by law.

9) How to Request a Refund Review

To request a refund review, contact support and include:

  • Account email
  • Company name (if applicable)
  • Invoice ID or Stripe receipt reference
  • Reason for request

10) Policy Relationship

This policy is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service. In the event of conflict, the Terms of Service controls unless prohibited by law.

This Refund & Billing Policy is provided for transparency and operational clarity. It does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
